2. **High (craft): a hard cut to an empty dark stage at 13.0 s.** `engine/src/jobs/ugc-01/film.json` ends S03 at 13.0; `engine/src/jobs/ugc-01/scenes/S04.tsx:28-31` wipes the light panel up from the bottom over an already empty stage. 4. **Medium: RecBadge is unreadable on the dark stage.** `engine/src/kit/ugc/ScreenRecTell.tsx:86` (`color: stage.onInk`): measured contrast 1.01 to 1 of the text against its pill on `stage: dark`. 5. **Medium: grade looks move skin hue past 5 deg** (`tools/footage/lib/grade.mjs:43-46` clean-cool: 7.3 deg and chroma collapse on two tones; `:47-50` warm-film 7.0 deg). The film's own recipe is clean-cool. Synthetic patches only.
