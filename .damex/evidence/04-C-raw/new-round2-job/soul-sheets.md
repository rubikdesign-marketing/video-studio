## B. Typed it again (RECOMMENDED, approved)
| | |
|---|---|
| Type | promo, web |
| Size and cap | 16:9, 30 s |
| Energy | confident: a launch whose news is a relief, not an emergency |

| Voice | Justin |

- One sentence: "Write the answer once and stop retyping it."
- Feeling: relief.
- One idea: the same reply is typed out again and again, then all the copies fold into one saved card.
- Signature moment: the repeats collapse into one card and "Saved replies" settles on it (about 3 s, a soft click, a held beat).
- Stage: light page, off-white and deep green; amber only on the saved card.
- Hook family: pain callout. Flow pattern: pain, reframe, promise.
- Never: 1. invented inbox screens (rule 10). 2. a claim about time saved or clicks that we cannot source. 3. people on screen (rule 23).
- Different from the last five: the ledger is empty, so nothing to repeat.
- New to the library: a move where repeated typed lines collapse into one card.
- Facts that must be right: offer wording "Saved replies". "Live today" needs the maker's confirmation.
