## One sentence
"Now I write an answer once and it's there when the question comes back."

## Feeling
Relief. The small tired feeling of repeating yourself, lifted.

## One idea
Repetition becomes one thing. The film opens on the same sentence being typed again and again down a stack of cards, then folds that stack into a single saved card that is used, edited and sent.

## Signature moment
Scene 3 to 4: the typed sentence folds into one labelled card, then that card flies into a reply, one word is changed, the question clears. The pointer and the card are the only things moving. Heard: three soft key ticks, then a settle tone on the fold.

## Stage
Night (dark green `#08130E`, bright green accent `#4CC38A`, amber `#F0B45A` for the saved card only). Inter throughout. Dark gives the glowing card something to glow against.

## Never
1. A made-up product screen presented as the real inbox.
2. Any claim about speed, hours saved or numbers. The brief gives none.
3. Dots, monospaced type, or people on screen (studio rules 20, 21, 23).
