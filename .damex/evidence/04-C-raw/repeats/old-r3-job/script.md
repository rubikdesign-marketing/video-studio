# Saved replies: idea, script and scene list

Demo brand (test brand), promo, 16:9, 30 s cap, confident, voice Justin (the only voice the brand has).
Status: waiting for the script approval. Nothing has been generated or played.

| | |
|---|---|
| One sentence | "They finally let me save my answers." |
| Feeling | Relief |
| One idea | The sentence you keep retyping gets written once. A stack of identical typed answers collapses into one saved reply. |

Two ideas I did not pick: a day-in-the-inbox countdown (needs real data I do not have) and a before/after split (reads as a comparison ad, which the brand has no proof for).

## Script

Voice is Justin: warm, trustworthy, clear. The demo brand has no written voice rules, so I used plain full sentences spoken to one person, outcomes first, and no feature jargon.

| Line | Spoken | Beat | Energy | Est. seconds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | You've typed that same answer a hundred times. | hook | 4 | 3.6 |
| 02 | Now you write it once. | turn | 3 | 2.0 |
| 03 | Save it as a reply, and it's waiting in your inbox for the next customer. | proof | 3 | 5.6 |
| 04 | Pick it, and the answer is ready to send. | peak | 5 | 3.2 |
| 05 | Saved replies, now in your support inbox. Save your first one. | close | 3 | 4.4 |

47 words, about 19 s of speech at 2.5 words a second, plus gaps of 0.7, 0.7, 0.8 and 1.0 s: about 22 s. That leaves about 8 s of the 30 s cap for the logo and holds. These are estimates, not measured timings.

## Three hooks (scored by my own judgement, not by the tool)

| | Spoken | On screen (7 words max) | First frame | Score /8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A, picked | You've typed that same answer a hundred times. | Same answer. Again. | One typed sentence appears, then stacks into copies down the page | 7 |
| B | Your best reply is buried in last week's inbox. | Where did I write that? | An inbox thread scrolling fast past a reply | 6 |
| C | What if you only had to answer once? | Answer once. | A single sentence, centred, still | 6 |

Hook A names a pain the viewer recognises, in the first 3 s, with words that do not repeat the voice. B and C are kept as ad variants. B would need a real inbox capture.
