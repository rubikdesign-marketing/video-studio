# Soul sheets: Saved replies promo (demo brand)

Three ideas, one recommended. The recommended one (A) is treated as approved by the maker.

Facts that hold for all three:
- The feature is called **saved replies**, in the support inbox. That wording is fixed and used identically everywhere.
- The demo brand is a test brand with no product, no captured screens and no discovery file. So no product screen is shown or claimed. Every picture is drawn type and shape on the brand's own colours. No numbers about time saved, no price, no date: none were given.
- 16:9, 30 s cap, five scenes, one voice-over (Justin, the brand's only voice), energy preset **confident** (launch).
- Brand: Inter only; deep green `#1F6B45` accent, amber `#C9822B` as the warm partner, off-white `#F6F4EC` page, dark green night `#08130E`. No people, no dots, no mono type.

---

## A. "The same answer, again" (RECOMMENDED)

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | promo (launch) |
| Where it runs | Product page, launch email, social |
| Size and length cap | 16:9, 30 s |
| Energy preset | confident (reason: a launch, not a sale; the claim is calm and sure) |
| Design style | Plain editorial type on a light page, closing into the dark green night page for the last scene (reason: type carries the idea, and no product UI exists to show) |
| Voice | Justin |
| Flow pattern | Repeat, then collapse: one thing happens many times, then once |
| Hook family | Callout / pain recognition, said as "you" |

**One sentence.** "They finally let you save the answer you keep typing."

**Feeling.** Relief, with a small smile.

**One idea.** The same sentence of reply text is typed out again and again on the page, each copy a little smaller and greyer, stacking down like a queue. Then one copy turns deep green and gets a name tag, "Saved reply". Every grey copy folds into it. What is left is one clean green line.

**Signature moment (about 3 s, scene 4).** The grey stack folds into the one green line with a soft ease, the line gets a small amber check, and the whole frame goes quiet for a beat. Heard: a single soft "tick", then the voice lands "Every customer gets your best answer."

**Stage.** A bare off-white page, like a blank reply box scaled up to the whole frame. It suits the idea because the story is about a typed sentence, and the page itself is the product's world. The last scene crosses to the dark green page for the logo.

**Never.**
1. Never show an invented product screen or inbox.
2. Never say or show a time-saved number, price or date.
3. Never show a person, or use dots, mono type or machine-sounding words.

**Different from the last five.** The brand's ledger is empty, so there is no earlier video to differ from. Hook: the callout family. Look: single-column type, no UI. Signature move: the stack folding into one line.

**New to the library.** A "stack fold" motion: repeated type lines compress into one line with a shared easing.

**References.** None studied. The brand has no study cards, and none were given.

**Facts that must be right.** Feature name "saved replies", location "support inbox". Source: the maker's brief. No other claims.

---

## B. "Pick one"

**One idea.** A single customer question sits left. Five different saved replies appear as quiet cards at right, and a selector slides to the right one. The reply fills in. Repeat with a second question, fast. The film is the choosing, not the typing.

**Hook family.** Demonstration (before and after): "Typed, then one click."

**Why not.** The cards are in effect an inbox screen. With no real product to capture, that is an invented screen. It would be the strongest idea once there is real footage, so keep it for the next episode.

**Feeling.** Control.

---

## C. "Slash"

**One idea.** Kinetic type. A short slash-word is typed ("/refund"), and it blows out into a whole paragraph of reply text, then collapses back and blows out again for another word. The film is about shorthand.

**Hook family.** Curiosity gap: "Four letters, and the whole answer is written."

**Why not.** It promises a slash-command, which the brief doesn't say the feature has. That is a product claim nobody has confirmed.

**Feeling.** Cleverness.

---

## Recommendation

A. It makes only the claim the brief gives us (saved replies exist in the support inbox), needs no product screen, and has one clear picture a viewer will remember. B comes back when we have real footage.
