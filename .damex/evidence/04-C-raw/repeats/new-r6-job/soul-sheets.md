## Recommended: A, "Once"

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | promo (launch reel) |
| Where it runs | The brand's site and social; no platform given |
| Size and length cap | 16:9, 30 s |
| Energy preset | confident (one reason: it is a convenience, not a sale) |
| Style | Paper, from `knowledge/craft/13-design-styles.md`. Light page, one green accent, ink type. Reason: the idea is a sentence, so type is the whole show |
| Voice | Justin (`brands/demo/voices.json`), warm and clear |

### One sentence
"Saved replies mean I write an answer once and use it again."

### Feeling
Relief.

### One idea
A sentence is written once. It becomes one card. The card is reused on the next ticket, and the one after. Everything is type and simple shapes; nothing is ever retyped on screen.

### Signature moment
About 20 s in. The same sentence sits in three ticket shapes at once: the three copies land together on one beat, with a soft tick on each and a hold of about a second. This is the three seconds worth replaying.

### Stage
Off-white paper with fine grain and a slow drift, so the film can rest. The accent green marks only the saved card. The warm amber is used once, on the repeated sentence in the opening, to feel like fatigue; it drains to ink when the card appears.

### Never
1. No invented product screen, and no claim about how the feature works that nobody confirmed (see Facts).
2. No people, filmed or generated (rule 23).
3. No numbers ("40 tickets", "twice as fast"), no hype words, no dots between words (rule 21).

### Different from the last five
The demo brand's ledger is empty, so there is nothing to repeat. Hook: a spoken problem. Look: Paper. Signature move: one sentence copied onto three tickets on one beat.

### New to the library
"One-to-many landing": one object's text duplicating onto several containers on a single beat, with a timed tick. (To be built at animation, and added to `library/`.)

### References
None. The demo brand has no study cards, and none were studied.
