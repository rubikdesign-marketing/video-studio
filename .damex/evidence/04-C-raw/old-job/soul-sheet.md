## One sentence
"They've added saved replies, so I never retype the same answer."

## Feeling
Relief: the repetitive part of the job just got lighter.

## One idea
"Write it well once." The same customer question lands again and again; the answer the viewer already wrote is simply there. The film is built on repetition, then the end of it.

## Signature moment
The reveal of saved replies, about seconds 19 to 22. Three near-identical customer messages in a stack; the empty reply box fills with the one saved answer in a single smooth move, and the stack clears. The product name sits large on the light page at the same moment. Sound: one soft, clean interface tick, no impact.

## Stage
Paper. The light page with fine grain, white windows, long soft shadows, one accent highlight. It keeps attention on the inbox, which is the whole product here.

## Never
1. No invented screens or data: only a real capture of the real feature (rule 10).
2. No claims about time saved or speed that the maker cannot source.
3. No people, no stock footage, no dots or monospaced type (rules 20, 21, 23).
