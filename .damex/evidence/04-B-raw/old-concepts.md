# Idea stage: three concepts for "ships the same day"

Common to all: promo, 9:16, 12 s cap, confident, no voice, library music, ends on the Demo logo. The ledger is empty, so there is no earlier video to differ from. At 12 s there is room for the logo at the close only; the promo playbook's "logo opens and closes" is dropped, and that needs your OK.

## A. "Order now. Out today."
Style: Paper (light). Hook family: Offer.
- One sentence: Orders placed today leave today.
- Feeling: quiet confidence.
- One idea: one parcel label whose date stamp reads "today" at the start and at the end. Nothing else changes.
- Signature moment (s 5-8): the stamp hits, the date reads TODAY, the label slides off.
- Never: confetti, truck clichés, a fake countdown.
- Energy: 4 / 2 / 5 / 3 / rest.
- New to library: a stamp hit with paper recoil.

## B. "Days, gone" (recommended)
Style: Night (dark green page, bright green accent, amber for the old wait). Hook family: Comparison.
- One sentence: Shipping used to take days. Now it's the same day.
- Feeling: relief, a small win.
- One idea: a row of days (MON TUE WED THU) with an amber wait that collapses into one bright green "TODAY" dot.
- Signature moment (s 4-7): the days fold into one point as the music lifts.
- Never: an invented delivery promise, a price, a real carrier logo.
- Energy: 5 / 2 / 5 / 3 / rest. Quiet beat right before the peak.
- New to library: a collapsing-timeline fold.

## C. "Beat the clock"
Style: Colour field (accent green edge to edge). Hook family: Pattern interrupt.
- One sentence: Order in time and it ships today.
- Feeling: mild urgency.
- One idea: a big clock face wipes to the line "Ships today".
- Signature moment: the clock hands snap into a stamp.
- Never: a cut-off time we have not been given.
- Risk: it implies a cut-off, which is a fact we don't have.
- New to library: a clock-hand snap.

## Recommendation: B
It reads with the sound off within 3 s, uses the brand's amber for the old way, and claims nothing beyond "same day".

2. OK to close on the logo only, with no opening logo.
3. Platform (Reels assumed).
