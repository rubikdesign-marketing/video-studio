## B. "Stamp"

**One idea:** A hand-stamp motif. A sheet reads "Ordered 9:00" and a green stamp slams down with "Shipped 9:00", the same time. The stamp's thud is the beat.
**Signature moment:** The stamp lands and the page shakes once.
**Hook family:** Pattern interrupt, the stamp arrives in the first second.
**Feeling:** Satisfaction.
**Risk:** "9:00" is an invented time, which would imply a cut-off we have not been given. It would need a neutral label such as "Ordered / Shipped" without numbers.
**Close:** logo mark settles where the stamp was.

## C. "Two Lines"

**One idea:** Pure kinetic type. Two lines: "Ordered this morning." / "Shipped this morning." The second line pushes the first off the screen, then collapses into "Same day."
**Signature moment:** The push, where "Ordered" and "Shipped" swap on the same baseline.
**Hook family:** Contrast, two words differing by one idea.
**Feeling:** Confidence, calm.
**Risk:** the plainest of the three, so the most dependent on the music and typography to feel like a reel.
**Close:** logo.

## Recommendation

**A, "The Clock".** It shows the claim (time passing, parcel already moving) instead of only stating it, it works with the sound off, and it needs no invented numbers or product screens. B risks implying a cut-off time. C is safe but the least memorable.
