# Same-day shipping reel: three concepts (Idea approval)

Settled by your words: 9:16, 12 s cap, no voice, library music only, ends on the logo. The demo brand has no product screens, so all three are drawn motion and type. Not shown, because nobody gave them: a cut-off time, price, region or carrier.

Common to all: confident energy; offer wording fixed as **"Same-day shipping"**; one library music track with hits on the cuts; words readable with sound off by 3 s.

## A. "Today" (recommended)
- **One sentence:** "They ship it the day you order it."
- **Feeling:** relief, a small win.
- **Idea:** one screen, one word. TODAY sits in frame; an order card lands on it, then the same card slides out the right edge as a parcel.
- **Hook family:** callout. First frame: "Ordered this morning." (0–3 s)
- **Signature moment (3–8 s):** the card turns into a parcel mid-move, a green tick stamps, a sun marker on a line travels morning to noon.
- **Close (8–12 s):** "Same-day shipping" holds, wipes to the logo, rests.
- **Stage and style:** light stage, editorial type-led.
- **Never:** invented screens or tracking numbers; a countdown with a made-up deadline; stock parcel photos.
- **New to library:** a card-to-parcel shared-element move.

## B. "Collapse"
- **One sentence:** "Order today, it's gone today."
- **Feeling:** brisk satisfaction.
- **Idea:** a row of day tiles collapses into one tile: today.
- **Hook family:** contrarian ("Waiting days for a parcel? Not here.")
- **Signature moment:** the whip-collapse of the tiles.
- **Risk:** it implies a "before" number we do not have. Needs a fact from you.
- **New to library:** a tile-collapse move.

## C. "Night shift"
- **One sentence:** "Even late orders go out the same day."
- **Feeling:** surprise.
- **Idea:** dark green stage, parcels as light dots on a line, daylight wipes in, logo lands.
- **Hook family:** curiosity ("What happens to your order in the next hour?")
- **Risk:** "late orders" claims a cut-off we do not have. Weakest on facts.
- **New to library:** a light-dots line animation.

## Recommendation
A. It makes the one claim you gave and nothing more, reads with sound off, and the logo end falls naturally on the wipe.
