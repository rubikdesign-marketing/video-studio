# Fix log: review findings applied to the recipe catalogue

Files changed: `synth/recipes.json`, `synth/17-motion-graphics.md`, `synth/brief-expansion.md`. The recipe changes were made by a script (kept in the session scratchpad as `fixwork/apply.js`) so every replacement was checked against the original text before it was made.

**What was checked afterwards.** `recipes.json` parses with `node -e "JSON.parse(...)"`. All 330 `engine_moves` entries across the 32 recipes exist in `engine-moves.tsv`. Every recipe has the same fields, in the same order, as before; so do `defaults`, `motion`, `structure` items and `references` items. Every `not_for` line points to a recipe id that exists. No trigger is shared by two recipes. The reviewer's 24 test briefs were re-run through the same exact and token matchers (`route.py`): 22 now match a trigger exactly (the particles brief and the conference-recap brief do not) and all 24 reach the intended recipe by exact match, token score or the written tie-breakers.

**What was not checked.** No film was rendered from the new starters. The new recipes have not been tried on a real brief. The table in section 5 of `17-motion-graphics.md` was not recomputed. The 4:5 size (1080x1350) and the alpha-channel export in graphics-package were not checked against the render tools.

**Schema decision.** The catalogue keeps its schema. Rules that apply to every recipe (routing, fallback, energy words, placeholder defaults, render line) went into the `about` text in labelled paragraphs (ROUTING, ENERGY, PLACEHOLDERS, RENDER) rather than into new keys. Placeholder defaults were not added as new keys inside `defaults` for the same reason.

**Recipe count:** 28 before, 32 after. app-promo was merged into product-launch-film. Five recipes were added: product-explainer, customer-story, quote-card, graphics-package and map-journey.
