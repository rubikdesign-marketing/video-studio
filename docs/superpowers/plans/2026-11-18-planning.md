# Planning (LIBRARY-PLAN phase 2) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A script can be laid out as a timed beat sheet that names its problems before anything is built; the shot list carries each scene's beat; a YouTube retention export can be read against the film's beats.

**Architecture:** Frameworks are data (`library/structure/frameworks.json`): the studio's own four playbook shapes plus the borrowed ones. Pure libraries under `tools/plan/lib/` (timing, beats, retention) with thin command-line tools beside them. The script gains two optional fields per line (`beat`, `energy`); the shot list gains an optional `beat` per scene. Nothing existing changes meaning.

**Tech Stack:** Node 22 (ESM, `node --test`). No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `LIBRARY-PLAN.md` (section 4 phase 2; "Calls already made": the retention reader is YouTube only, marked untested until it reads a real export).

## Global Constraints

- Speaking pace 2.5 words a second (stage 2). On-screen text: read at 3 words a second, held for words ÷ 3 + 0.4 s (`04-type-and-captions.md`).
- The energy map rules are `01-direction.md`'s, word for word: never open at 3; never the same number for more than three beats; a 1 or 2 directly before the biggest moment; the highest number once; end one step below the peak.
- The playbooks' shapes are the default frameworks for their film types; borrowed frameworks are alternatives, credited.
- Real line times come from `audio/lines.json` when it exists; otherwise they are estimated and the output says so.
- Plain words in every message a person reads. No statistic from a source without its source.

## Review Focus

- A script with no `beat` fields: beats are assigned by time, and the sheet says they were guessed.
- A line whose `beat` is not in the framework: named as a problem, not dropped.
- A film shorter or longer than every length a framework was written for: the shares still apply, and the sheet says the framework was stretched.
- A retention export with positions in percent, in seconds, or with a header row the parser does not expect: either read correctly or refused with the reason.
- A retention export longer than the film (or the wrong film): refused when its length differs from the film's by more than 10%.

### Task 3: The beat sheet

**Files:** Create `tools/plan/lib/beats.mjs`, `tools/plan/beats.mjs`, `tools/plan/tests/beats.test.mjs`.

**Interfaces — Produces:**
- `assignBeats(lines, framework, duration) -> lines with beat and how ('given' | 'by time')`
- `beatSheet({ lines, framework, duration, cap, platform }) -> { beats: [{ id, name, lines, start, end, seconds, window: [s, e], verdict }], problems: [{ what, beat|line, detail }], guessed: boolean, stretched: boolean }`. Problems: `hook late` (the hook beat ends after its `max_seconds`, or after 3 s on reels, tiktok and shorts), `beat long`, `beat short` (outside what it is allowed by more than a tenth of its longest allowed length, and at least 0.5 s), `beat missing` (a required beat with no line), `out of order`, `unknown beat`, `over the cap`, `line long` (a line over 6 s to say).
- `energyProblems(values) -> problems` (the five rules; values per beat in order).
- `textHolds(shots, sceneSeconds) -> problems` (`text too short on screen` when a shot's text has less time than words ÷ 3 + 0.4 s).
- CLI: `beats.mjs jobs/<job> [--framework <id>] [--platform <p>] [--json]` reads `audio/script.json`, `audio/lines.json` if present, `job.json` (type, `length_cap_seconds`), `shots.json` if present (scene `beat`, `energy`, shot texts); prints the sheet as a table and the problems; exit 0 with no problems, 1 with problems, 2 when it cannot run. `beats.mjs frameworks` lists them.

- [ ] Tests for each problem kind on made-up scripts; the energy rules on four shapes (good, opens at 3, flat for four beats, two peaks); a 30 s promo script with no beats assigned by time.
- [ ] Implement, run, commit.
