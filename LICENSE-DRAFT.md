# Video Studio licence (draft)

**Draft for a lawyer to review. Not legal advice and not in force.** Written 11 Nov 2026 from the owner's decisions: other companies may use and change the studio for their own business, but may not resell it or pass it on. Fill in the names in square brackets before use.

---

## Who this is between

[Licensor: the company that owns the Video Studio] ("we") and [Licensee: the company receiving it] ("you").

## What you may do

1. Use the Video Studio to make videos for your own business and your own products.
2. Change it in any way: add your brand, your rules, your animations, your tools.
3. Let your own employees and contractors use it, while they work for you.
4. Keep and use every video you make with it, for any purpose, with no fee to us. The videos are yours.

## What you may not do

1. Sell, rent or license the Video Studio, or anything built mainly from it, to anyone else.
2. Give or pass on a copy, changed or not, to anyone outside your company.
3. Offer it as a service to other companies (for example, making videos with it for clients as an agency) without our written agreement.
4. Remove this licence or the notice of who made the studio.

## What is not ours to give

The studio uses tools and material that belong to others. Their own terms apply, and you must accept them yourself:

- **Remotion** (the video engine) has its own licence. A company with more than three people needs a Remotion company licence, bought from Remotion.
- **ElevenLabs** (voice, music and sound effects) needs your own account and key. What you may do with the sound it makes is set by your ElevenLabs plan.
- **ffmpeg**, **whisper** and the other programs the installer adds keep their own open-source licences.
- **Fonts** in the starter are under the SIL Open Font License. Fonts you add are under their own terms.
- **Reference videos** belong to the companies that made them. The studio lists links to them and our notes about them; it does not give you the videos. Download them only where their owners' terms allow, and study them; never place any part of them in your own videos.

## Our notes and know-how

The craft notes, study cards, rules, playbooks and animations we wrote are part of the Video Studio. You may use and change them inside your company under the same terms as above.

## No promises

The Video Studio is given as it is. We do not promise it will work for you, be free of faults, or keep working with future versions of the tools it uses. We are not responsible for any loss that comes from using it, or for the videos you make with it. You are responsible for what your videos show and say, and for having the rights to everything in them.

## Ending

If you break these terms, your right to use the studio ends, and you must delete your copies. Videos you made before then stay yours.
