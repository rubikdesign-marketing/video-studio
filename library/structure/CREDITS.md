# Credits for the structure library

`frameworks.json` holds the studio's own four playbook shapes (the `studio-` frameworks) and frameworks taken on 18 Nov 2026 from the open-source projects below, with their beat timings turned into sizes. The rules in `knowledge/craft/16-structure-and-retention.md` come from the same projects. No statistic or claim about results was taken from any of them.

Copyright 2026 Yuri Strohm / WhyStrohm |
