# Credits for the hook library

The formulas in `index.json` and the rules in `knowledge/craft/15-hooks.md` were gathered on 18 Nov 2026 from the open-source projects below, then merged, de-duplicated and reworded for this studio. Every example line was written fresh. Each formula's `source` names where its pattern came from. No statistic, view count or claim about results was taken from any of them.
