## Replication lessons and tradeoffs

For a beginner, the strongest reusable idea is to make a broad AI claim tangible through a series of named, task-specific examples. Use one short screen capture per agent, show the agent name before scrolling through its setup, and keep the host's gestures directed toward the overlay. Keep descriptions short enough to read at mobile size and use a zoomed crop for the one piece of evidence that proves each capability. If six examples are promised, make all six legible and give the final example a clear landing before the close. The current visual approach feels personable and practical, but the copy density and narrow UI reduce legibility. A visible caption layer would also help viewers follow the story with sound off.
