# Outside research: what to add to the local video findings

Reviewed on 20 October 2026. Exa returned 35 ranked candidate entries across seven search workstreams: TikTok creative, Google/YouTube creative, Wistia business video data, W3C accessibility, and three company product overviews. Candidates were screened for relevance and primary ownership; duplicate results were not counted as independent corroboration. Five guidance pages were fetched for additional context. This is targeted supplementary research, not an exhaustive review of all SaaS video literature.

## Additions worth testing beyond the supplied clips

These are recommendations derived from the sources and production judgment, not techniques proven effective by this sample:

1. **Pair attention with reproducibility.** After the entertaining hook, show enough of the prompt, starting state and final output that the viewer can repeat the task. Use an accompanying longer tutorial when the feed edit is too short.
2. **Treat audio as product evidence.** For a voice tool, use the same short input across alternatives, identify the output, and let the viewer compare. Keep any processing or edits transparent. This local still-frame study cannot rank voice quality.
3. **Make results auditable.** Label time compression, remove private information before capture, and distinguish a staged demo from real customer evidence. Never use a fabricated benchmark as proof.
4. **Make the CTA continue the demonstration.** Link to the matching template, sample project, or starter workflow instead of a generic homepage when that better matches intent.
5. **Check both muted and sound-on comprehension.** Product outcome, brand and next step should remain legible; sound adds information without being the only route to the message.
6. **Test creative variables deliberately.** Compare two hooks on the same body, then test proof placement or CTA. Measure qualified visits and activation as well as retention; platform definitions differ.
7. **Build a connected content ladder.** A short clip can earn interest, a walkthrough can explain the work, and a customer case can address purchase risk. One asset does not need to accomplish all three.

## Evidence discipline

No performance metadata accompanies the 31 files. An attractive technique is not a proven conversion driver. Platform guidance supplies useful starting hypotheses; the company's own audience and funnel data decide what to keep. This research does not repeat platform uplift statistics as promised outcomes.
