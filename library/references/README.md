# Reference films

The studio studies other companies' films to learn principles, timings and moves. Nothing from a reference is ever placed in a studio video.

The films themselves are **not in this repository**: they belong to the companies that made them. What is kept here is ours: `index.json` (each film's name, source and measurements), the profiles in `_profiles/`, and the study cards.

To get the films on your Mac, ask the owner for the team's private folder and copy its contents into `library/references/`, keeping the same file names. Or, for films with a link in `index.json`, download them yourself with `yt-dlp` where their owners' terms allow. Without the files, everything still works except re-measuring a film.
