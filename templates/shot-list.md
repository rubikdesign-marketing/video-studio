## Checks on the list before any key frame is built

- [ ] Total length is at or under the cap.
- [ ] Energy follows the rules in `craft/01-direction.md`: no three beats alike, a rest before the peak, one peak.
- [ ] No two neighbouring shots share a shot size and layout.
- [ ] No two neighbouring transitions are the same technique; at most five techniques in the film.
- [ ] Every scene change has a hand-off object or a stated reason.
- [ ] Focal point moves less than a quarter of the frame across each change, or something carries it.
- [ ] Every capture file exists and comes from the real product.
- [ ] On-screen text is at most seven words per shot and matches the approved wording.
- [ ] Planned holds are marked (at most two proof holds of about 1.3 s).
- [ ] The signature moment from the soul sheet is in the list, at the peak.
- [ ] Beats for the 30 s, 15 s and 6 s versions are marked (promos only).
