# Product discovery: <brand>, <product>

Filled in at stage 0B (`stages/00b-product-discovery.md`) before the brand's first video, and refreshed when the product, the pricing or the audience changes. Saved as `brands/<brand>/product/discovery.md`, with sources in `brands/<brand>/product/discovery-sources/`.

Rules for filling it in:
- Every fact has a source and a date (a page, a review, a call, a person). No source, no fact.
- Customers' words are quoted as they wrote them, with where they wrote them. They are gold for scripts.
- Mark each claim **verified** (checked in the product or a public page today), **company says** (told by the team, not checked) or **guess** (ours, to confirm).
- Plain words. Sentence case. No dots as separators.
