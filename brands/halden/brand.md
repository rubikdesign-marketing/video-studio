# Demo brand, for video

A test brand. The numbers are in `brand.json`; `README.md` says why this folder exists.

| Role | Light page | Dark page |
|---|---|---|
| Page | Off-white `#F6F4EC` | Dark green `#08130E` |
| Ink | `#10201A` | `#F3F5EE` |
| Second clause, supporting text | `#6F7A72` | `#A8CBB5` |
| The accent | Deep green `#1F6B45` | Bright green `#4CC38A` |
| Warm partner | Amber `#C9822B` | Amber `#F0B45A` |
| Soft fill | `#DDEBDD` | `#12392A` |
| Lines | `#DAD9CC` | Off-white at 14% |

Type: Inter for headlines and labels. No accent typeface and no product typeface.
