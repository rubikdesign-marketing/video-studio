# Tulvo's rules

Rules that apply to Tulvo videos only. Rules for every brand's videos live in `knowledge/rules/`.

## Where Tulvo's rules are today

Tulvo's rules have not been split out yet. They are in `knowledge/rules/starting-rules.md`, approved on 11 Nov 2026 from 34 rounds of feedback on the Black Friday and UGC work. That file mixes two kinds of rule:

- Rules about craft that hold for any brand (no jumps, the length cap, the voice is never stretched).
- Rules that are Tulvo's own. At the time of writing these are rule 11 (stay in blue, ice blue and warm peach; no purple, pink or ash), rule 15 ("Tulvo" is said TOOL-voh) and the Tulvo wording in rules 7, 20 and 21, which the owner set for Tulvo on 11 Nov 2026.

Until they are moved here, read `knowledge/rules/starting-rules.md` for every Tulvo video.

## Also binding for Tulvo

- The rejected colours, patterns and type in `brand.json`, under "rejected".
- The "Decided" list in `brand.md`.
- `pronunciation.md` for how product words are written in voice scripts.

## Adding a rule

A new Tulvo rule starts as a lesson in `lessons/`. It moves here once the owner approves it, with the date and the feedback it came from.
