# Lessons from the Product Hunt launch reel (13 Nov 2026)

Proposed, not rules yet. Each needs the owner's yes before it moves to `../rules.md` or the studio's rules.

1. **Presenter footage is allowed for Tulvo when the owner asks for it.** Rule 23 (no filmed people) was set before the footage lane existed. This reel used the hired content intern, with her agreement for posting and ads. Proposed: rule 23 becomes "no generated people; filmed people only with recorded consent".
2. **The logo can stay on screen for a whole social reel.** The owner asked for it on this video (rule 13 says opening and closing only). Proposed: allowed for 9:16 social edits, small, top left inside the safe band.
3. **Words behind the presenter's head work when they sit high.** Centred at the hairline, a head hides too many letters. Centred just above it (8 to 30% of the face height, more for close shots), only the bottom of two or three letters tucks behind the hair and the word still reads.
4. **Tulvo's onboarding form ("Tell us about your work") locks its Work Type and Description fields when typed into by the capture tool,** so it makes an empty workspace. The Tulvo AI chat panel builds real templates and boards from one sentence. Use the chat for "describe it, Tulvo builds it" shots.
5. **Tulvo AI's first plan can be generic and can include the account owner's name** (it listed "Staff involved" with the owner's real name). Always mask the owner's name in AI captures, and read every generated word before using the take.
6. **ElevenLabs music can end early.** The "Close" section asked for 33.4 to 37.8 s ended at about 32 s. Check the music's loudness over time before mixing; repeating whole bars on the beat is a clean fix.
7. **Record Tulvo for 9:16 at phone size (390 x 844).** Tulvo switches to its mobile layout below about 768 px wide: Tulvo AI opens full screen with its header, and text lands at about 39 px in a 1080 x 1920 frame. A desktop recording zoomed into a reel gives 15 to 22 px text, too small to read on a phone.
8. **Tulvo AI can time out** ("Tool timed out after 120s"); retry in a new conversation. It can also make duplicate cards on a board; check the board before filming it.
9. **The Tulvo AI panel's open or closed state is remembered by the browser**, so a click that opens it in one take closes it in the next. Check the state at the start of every take.
10. **Captions for reels: short, bold, word by word, no pill.** The studio's glass pill looked heavy at 9:16 and its line changes overlapped. Proposed for 9:16 edits; the owner approved the change for this reel on 13 Nov 2026.
